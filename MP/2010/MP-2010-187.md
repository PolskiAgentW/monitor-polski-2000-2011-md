> Understanding between the Government of the Republic of Poland and the Government of the Republic of Indonesia on the amendment to the Agreement between the Government of the Republic of Poland and the Government of the Republic of Indonesia on extending a credit done in Jakarta on March 2, 2007 amended by the Understanding dated July 3, 2009

> The Government of the Republic of Poland and the Government of the Republic of Indonesia, henceforth called the “Contracting Parties” with a view to continue the constructive economic co-operation between the Republic of Poland and the Republic of Indonesia, have agreed as follows:

> In accordance with Article 10 of the Agreement between the Government of the Republic of Poland and the Government of the Republic of Indonesia on extending a credit, done in Jakarta on March 2, 2007, the Contracting Parties have agreed that Article 1 paragraph 3 of this Agreement will have the wording:

> *Article 1.3

> The Credit shall be utilised by December 31, 2010 and this term may be extended upon mutual consent of both Contracting Parties.”.

> Article 2 The Understanding shal] be approved in consistence with laws of each Contracting Party, and shail enter into force on the date of signing.
